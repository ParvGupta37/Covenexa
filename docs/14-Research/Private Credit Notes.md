# Private Credit Domain Notes

## Deal Structures

### First Lien Term Loan B (TLB)
- Senior secured, first priority
- Floating rate: SOFR + 400–600 bps typically
- 7-year term, amortizes 1% annually
- Majority of Covenexa's target borrowers

### Unitranche
- Single loan combining first and second lien economics
- Simpler documentation for middle market
- Higher yield to lender; simpler for borrower

### Revolving Credit Facility (Revolver)
- Committed but undrawn capacity
- Interest only when drawn
- Springing financial covenants: only tested when drawn > X%

### Second Lien / Mezzanine
- Junior to first lien; higher yield
- More common in leveraged buyouts

## Fund Economics

- Management fee: 1.5–2% of committed capital
- Carry (performance fee): 15–20% of profits above preferred return
- Preferred return (hurdle): 6–8%
- GP commitment: typically 1–5% of fund size

## Private Credit vs. Bank Loans

| Feature | Private Credit | Syndicated Bank Loan |
|:--------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| Lenders | 1–3 direct lenders | 50–200 banks |
| Speed | 2–4 weeks | 6–12 weeks |
| Flexibility | High (bespoke docs) | Lower (standard docs) |
| Covenant package | Tighter, maintenance covenants | Looser, cov-lite |
| Interest rate | Higher (private risk premium) | Lower |
| Confidentiality | Full | SEC filing required if public borrower |

## Covenant Maintenance vs. Covenant-Lite (Cov-Lite)

- **Maintenance covenants:** tested every quarter regardless of draw — Covenexa monitors these
- **Incurrence covenants:** only tested when a specific event occurs (new debt, acquisition)
- **Cov-lite:** institutional term loans with no maintenance covenants — less relevant for middle market direct lending
