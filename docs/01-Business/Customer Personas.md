# Customer Personas

## Portfolio Manager

### Responsibilities

- Manage loan portfolios
- Monitor borrower performance
- Make investment decisions

### Pain Points

- Limited visibility across portfolios
- Delayed identification of risky borrowers

---

## Credit Analyst

### Responsibilities

- Review loan agreements
- Analyze borrower financial statements
- Monitor covenant compliance

### Pain Points

- Manual document review
- Time-consuming calculations
- Repetitive reporting

---

## Risk Manager

### Responsibilities

- Evaluate portfolio risk
- Monitor exposure
- Identify early warning signals

### Pain Points

- Lack of predictive insights
- Difficult portfolio-wide analysis

---

## Compliance Officer

### Responsibilities

- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Maintain audit records
- Verify reporting obligations

### Pain Points

- Manual tracking
- Audit preparation effort
