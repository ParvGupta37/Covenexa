# Competitive Analysis

## Existing Solutions

- Loan management software
- Document management systems
- Financial reporting tools
- Spreadsheet-based workflows

## Limitations

- Manual covenant monitoring
- Limited AI capabilities
- Disconnected systems
- Reactive instead of proactive risk management

## Covenexa's Differentiators

- AI-native architecture
- Multi-agent intelligence
- Automated covenant extraction
- Explainable AI with citations
- Borrower Health Score
- Default prediction
- Portfolio stress testing
- AI-powered recommendations
- Enterprise-ready security and governance

## Vision

Covenexa is designed to become the AI intelligence layer for private credit, transforming static documents and financial data into continuous portfolio insights.
