# User Journey

## Step 1

User uploads:

- Loan Agreement
- Amendments
- Financial Statements
- Compliance Certificates

↓

## Step 2

AI reads and understands every document.

↓

## Step 3

Covenants and financial definitions are extracted into a structured knowledge base.

↓

## Step 4

Financial statements are analyzed and mapped to agreement definitions.

↓

## Step 5

The Compliance Engine evaluates every covenant.

↓

## Step 6

The Portfolio Intelligence Engine calculates:

- Borrower Health Score
- Default Probability
- Portfolio Risk

↓

## Step 7

The AI Copilot answers user questions and explains every decision with citations.

↓

## Step 8

Reports, dashboards, and alerts are generated automatically.
