# Private Credit Explained

## What is Private Credit?

Instead of borrowing money from a bank, companies borrow directly from private investment firms.

The lender provides capital, and the borrower agrees to repay it according to a legal agreement.

## Loan Lifecycle

1. Borrower requests a loan.
2. Lender evaluates the company.
3. Loan Agreement is signed.
4. Borrower submits periodic financial reports.
5. Lender monitors covenant compliance.
6. Loan is repaid or refinanced.

## What are Covenants?

Covenants are rules that borrowers must follow during the loan period.

Examples:

- Maintain a minimum liquidity level.
- Keep debt below a specified limit.
- Submit financial reports every quarter.

If a covenant is violated, it is called a **Covenant Breach**.

## Why Monitoring Matters

Monitoring helps lenders identify financial deterioration early and take corrective action before losses occur.
