# Industry Overview

## What is Private Credit?

Private credit is a form of lending where investment firms lend money directly to businesses without involving traditional banks.

These loans are usually provided to medium and large companies for expansion, acquisitions, refinancing, or working capital.

## Industry Participants

- Private Credit Funds
- Direct Lending Funds
- Asset Managers
- Institutional Investors
- Borrowers
- Legal Advisors

## Current Challenges

- Manual review of loan agreements
- Complex covenant monitoring
- Delayed risk identification
- Large volumes of legal and financial documents
- Time-consuming portfolio reporting
