# Industry Overview — Private Credit

## What is Private Credit?

Private credit (also called direct lending or private debt) refers to loans made by non-bank institutions — private equity funds, asset managers, insurance companies — directly to companies, bypassing traditional bank financing and public bond markets.

Since the 2008 financial crisis, banks have pulled back from middle-market lending due to regulatory capital requirements (Basel III). This created a $1.5 trillion+ market opportunity that private credit funds stepped in to fill.

---

## Market Size & Growth

- Global private credit AUM: ~$1.6 trillion (2024)
- Growing at 15–20% annually
- Largest players: Ares, Apollo, Blackstone, Blue Owl, HPS, Golub Capital
- Middle market focus: companies with $10M–$100M EBITDA

---

## How Private Credit Works

1. **Fund raises capital** from LPs (pension funds, endowments, sovereign wealth funds)
2. **Fund deploys capital** by making direct loans to portfolio companies
3. **Borrower receives loan** — typically floating rate, 5–7 year term
4. **Borrower makes quarterly interest payments** + repays principal at maturity
5. **Covenants are monitored** to ensure borrower remains financially healthy
6. **Default risk** is managed via covenant breach detection and early intervention

---

## Types of Private Credit

| Type | Description |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Direct Lending** | First lien senior secured loans — lowest risk, highest priority in default |
| **Mezzanine** | Junior/subordinated debt — higher yield, lower priority |
| **Unitranche** | Single loan combining senior and mezzanine — simpler for borrowers |
| **Distressed Debt** | Buying stressed or defaulted loans at a discount |
| **Real Estate Debt** | Loans secured by commercial real estate |
| **Infrastructure Debt** | Long-duration loans for infrastructure assets |

## Why Covenant Monitoring Matters

Loan agreements contain **financial covenants** — contractual obligations the borrower must maintain. Examples:
- Total Debt / EBITDA ≤ 4.0x
- Interest Coverage Ratio ≥ 2.0x
- Current Ratio ≥ 1.2x

**Covenant maintenance** requires lenders to:
- Review quarterly/annual financial statements from each borrower
- Calculate each covenant ratio
- Compare to threshold
- Escalate if a breach is detected

For a fund managing 30–100 borrowers, this is an enormous manual burden — and the cost of missing a breach is high (both financial and reputational).
