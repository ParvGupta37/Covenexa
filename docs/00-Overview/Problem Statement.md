# Problem Statement

Private credit firms manage hundreds of loan agreements, each containing unique financial covenants and reporting obligations.

Monitoring these agreements is largely manual.

As portfolios grow, teams spend significant time:

- Reviewing legal agreements
- Tracking covenant compliance
- Analyzing borrower financial statements
- Preparing portfolio reports
- Identifying early warning signals

Manual processes are slow, expensive, and prone to human error, increasing the risk of missed covenant breaches and delayed decisions.

Covenexa solves this by automating document understanding, financial analysis, covenant monitoring, and portfolio intelligence using AI.
