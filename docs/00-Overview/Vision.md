# Vision

## Product Vision

Covenexa is an AI-powered covenant intelligence platform that helps private credit firms monitor loan agreements, analyze borrower financial health, predict potential defaults, and proactively identify covenant risks before they become costly problems.

## Mission

Enable lenders to replace manual covenant monitoring with AI-driven, real-time risk intelligence.

## Long-Term Goal

Become the AI operating system for private credit portfolios by combining document intelligence, financial analytics, knowledge graphs, and autonomous AI agents into one platform.

## Target Users

- Private Credit Funds
- Direct Lenders
- Credit Analysts
- Portfolio Managers
- Risk Teams

## Core Value Proposition

Instead of manually reviewing hundreds of loan agreements and financial reports, Covenexa continuously analyzes portfolio data and alerts lenders to emerging risks with actionable recommendations.

## Guiding Principles

- AI assists, humans decide.
- Every insight must be explainable.
- Financial calculations remain deterministic.
- Security and compliance come first.
- Design for enterprise scale.
