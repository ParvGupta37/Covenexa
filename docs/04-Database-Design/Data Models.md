# Data Models

## Core Business Entities

### Organization

Owns one or more borrowers.

---

### Borrower

Represents the company receiving the loan.

---

### Loan

Financial agreement between lender and borrower.

---

### Agreement

Legal contract containing all loan terms and covenants.

---

### Covenant

Financial or operational condition that the borrower must satisfy.

---

### Financial Statement

Periodic financial report submitted by the borrower.

---

### Compliance Result

Stores the outcome of covenant evaluations.

---

### Borrower Health Score

AI-generated score representing borrower financial health.

---

### Default Prediction

Estimated probability that the borrower may default.

---

### Portfolio

Collection of loans monitored by the lender.

---

### Recommendation

AI-generated action suggested to the credit team.
